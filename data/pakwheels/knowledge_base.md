# PakWheels support knowledge base (unofficial demo)

Paraphrased from public pages on pakwheels.com, checked on 2026-10-01. Prices, fees,
cities and phone numbers change: re-check each section's source page before a demo.
Each "## " section is one retrieval chunk; keep every section under 1,000 characters.
Bank account numbers are deliberately NOT included: the assistant must always send
customers to the official How To Pay page instead of quoting account details.

## Contact and support hours

Human support hours are Monday to Sunday, 9:00 am to 9:00 pm. Outside these hours the
virtual assistant can still answer general questions, and customers can leave a message
through the Contact Us form (subjects include account, ads, blog, forums, feedback,

Liberty Market, Gulberg, Lahore.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/main/contact_us

## Featured ad prices
A featured ad stays at the top of search results and on the Used Cars main page and gets
a Featured tag. Listed single-ad prices: 7 days PKR 2,950; 14 days PKR 4,450; 28 days
PKR 5,950. Bundles of feature credits are cheaper per ad (for example 5 credits for
PKR 13,450 and 10 credits for PKR 25,450). Prices may change; the Feature Your Ad page
shows the current list.
Common questions: how much does a featured ad cost, feature my ad for a week or a month,
feature ad paise, feature ad qeemat, charges to boost or feature an ad.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/products/feature_your_ad

## How to feature an ad and pay
Post the ad first and note its Ad ID from the ad page. Pay using one of the methods on
the official How To Pay section of the Feature Your Ad page (crossed cheque in favour of
Pak Wheels (Pvt.) Limited, or online deposit to the accounts listed there). Then call
042-111-943-357 with the payment details. The ad is featured once payment is confirmed.
Never send money to a personal account or to anyone who contacts you claiming to be
PakWheels; use only the details shown on the official page.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/products/feature_your_ad#how_to_pay

## Sell It For Me fees
Fees for Sell It For Me, the service where PakWheels sells your car for you. Sell It For
Me charges a non-refundable onboarding fee at sign-up: PKR 2,000 up to 1000cc, PKR 5,000
for 1001cc to 2000cc, and PKR 7,000 for SUVs, 4x4s, jeeps and German cars. After the car
is sold, PakWheels charges a 1% commission on the selling price, with a minimum
commission of PKR 5,000 when the car sells for 5 lakh or less.
Common questions: Sell It For Me charges, sign-up fee, commission on the sale price,
Sell It For Me ki fee aur commission kitna hai.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/products/pakwheels-sell-it-for-me/

## Sell It For Me eligibility and process
Available in Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Peshawar.
The car must be model year 2000 or newer; open-letter cars are not eligible. Process:
sign up, the team visits to photograph and inspect the car and posts a featured ad,
a dedicated sales rep handles calls, offers and negotiation (aiming to close a deal within
45 days), and PakWheels handles paperwork and secure payment. To buy a Managed by
PakWheels car, call 0423-2560407.
Common questions: is Sell It For Me available in my city, is my car eligible.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/products/pakwheels-sell-it-for-me/

## Car inspection coverage and process
A 200+ point check by trained inspectors: computer scan for engine fault codes, paint
depth for accident repairs, suspension, road test, electricals, interior, AC and body
frame. Imported Japanese cars are cross-checked against the auction sheet. It takes about
45 to 90 minutes; the verified report (with a QR code) arrives by SMS and email, usually
within about an hour after manager review. An inspection is an assessment at that time,
not a guarantee. To reschedule, contact support.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/products/pakwheels-inspection/

## Car inspection cities
Inspection is available in 12 cities: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar,
Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Gujrat, Hyderabad, Multan, Sargodha and Sialkot. In Lahore it can
be done at the PakWheels Inspection Center or at your location; in the other cities at
your preferred location, including the seller's location, subject to availability.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/products/pakwheels-inspection/

## Inspected vs Certified cars
PakWheels Inspection only reports the condition of the car that was inspected; it does
not approve the car. PakWheels Certified cars are inspected on 200+ points, must be
non-accidental with a good engine and gearbox, and come with a free repair warranty.
Both can be filtered in Used Cars search ("PakWheels Inspected" and "PakWheels Certified").
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/pakwheels-certify

## Auction sheet verification
For imported Japanese cars. Open the Auction Sheet Verification page, enter the chassis
number and your email, pay the fee shown at checkout (the amount is not listed in this
knowledge base), and the original auction sheet is sent by SMS or email. It can include the auction grade, mileage, colour, auction date and
sold price. Sheets are delivered as-is, without edits. Records go back roughly 14 years of
imports; if a record is not found online, PakWheels checks with the auction houses. Help:
042-111-943-357.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/auction-sheet-verification/

## Car registration and ownership transfer service
PakWheels offers car registration and ownership transfer services in Lahore, Karachi and
Islamabad. After you apply online, a PakWheels service advisor calls to confirm the
details and required documents, you can track progress online, and the advisor arranges
delivery of your documents. Buyers should avoid cars sold on an open transfer letter.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/products/car-registration/

## Car finance
The Car Finance page has a car loan calculator that compares partner banks' rates,
markup and insurance for new and used cars. You can apply on PakWheels and your request
is shared with partner banks. The page's FAQ lists a minimum down payment of 35%; the
final terms, eligibility and documents are decided by the bank.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/car-finance/

## Car insurance
PakWheels offers car insurance. The Car Insurance page compares quotes for new, used and
imported cars from partner insurance companies such as Adamjee, EFU, Jubilee, IGI and
TPL. PakWheels does not charge customers for this service; you pay the insurer's premium.
Service is offered in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad, with nationwide coverage through
partner companies.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/car-insurance/

## AutoStore returns and refunds
Returns are accepted for damaged or defective, incomplete, wrong (size, colour, model,
fake or expired) items, or items that don't match the description. Change of mind is not
accepted for car or bike accessories. Start the return within 3 business days (72 hours)
of receiving the order. Replacements ship in 5 to 10 working days; approved refunds are
paid by bank deposit within 7 to 10 working days.
Common questions: I received the wrong item, my order arrived damaged or broken, can I
return or exchange something I ordered (mats, covers, lights), what do I do, refund.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/accessories-spare-parts/refunds-returns-policy/

## AutoStore payment methods

MasterCard and UnionPay cards. Businesses can apply to become AutoStore vendors through

Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/accessories-spare-parts/refunds-returns-policy/

## Posting, closing and reopening ads
Posting an ad for a car, bike or accessory is free: sign in, choose Post an Ad / Sell
Your Car, fill in the form and submit. Ads are reviewed before going live; you are told
once approved. To close an ad, open My Ads and choose Close; to bring back a closed or
expired ad, open it again from My Ads (it is reviewed again). Ads may be rejected for
incomplete or wrong details, pictures or text that break the terms, or if PakWheels
could not reach you by phone to verify.
Common questions: I want to sell my car, how to post an ad, gari bechna, car sell karna,
ad lagana, is posting free, remove a sold car's ad.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/FAQ

## Account, login and privacy
You can sign in with a mobile number (OTP), Google, Facebook or email. Activation email
missing: check spam, then try signing in and use the resend activation link. Forgot
password: on Sign In choose Forgot Password, enter your email and follow the reset link.
Your email is not shown to buyers; their messages are forwarded to you. Never share your
OTP or password with anyone, including people claiming to be PakWheels staff.
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/FAQ
